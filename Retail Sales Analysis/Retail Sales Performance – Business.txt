Retail Sales Performance – Business Insights Report

This report summarizes the analysis performed on the retail transaction dataset and highlights key insights, observations, and 
strategic recommendations derived from the dashboard.

1. Analysis Overview

A retail transactions dataset containing 500 records was analyzed to understand sales performance across regions, products, 
categories, and sales channels. SQL was used for data preparation and aggregation, while Power BI was used to design 
an interactive one-page dashboard.

• Calculated key KPIs such as Total Revenue, Total Transactions, Total Customers, and Average Order Value.
• Analyzed monthly sales trends to identify seasonality and peak periods.
• Evaluated region-wise and sales-channel-wise contributions to total revenue.
• Identified top-performing products and monitored category-wise performance trends.

2. Key Business Insights

• Stable Overall Revenue: Sales show consistent performance with noticeable peaks during mid-year, indicating seasonal demand.
• Balanced Regional Contribution: All regions contribute relatively evenly to total revenue.
• Revenue Concentration: Top products account for a large portion of overall sales.
• Channel Performance: Online and offline channels complement each other, with online performing better in certain months.
• Category Dynamics: Category-level trends suggest changing customer preferences.

3. Future Recommendations

• Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing months and products.
• Strengthen online sales initiatives and digital promotions.
• Use customer purchase behavior to design loyalty and retention programs.
• Monitor underperforming categories and adjust pricing or assortment strategies.
• Continue tracking KPIs through dashboards for ongoing performance evaluation.

4. Conclusion

The dashboard enables management to quickly assess sales health and make informed decisions. Regular monitoring of these insights 
will help optimize revenue growth and customer satisfaction.
